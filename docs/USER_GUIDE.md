## 🧭 Market State Assessment: The Quick-Check Playbook & 10-Second Triage Routine

To assess market state in the **Cubera GEX Cockpit**, read the **call−put book (GEX)** next to **tape (CVD / Orderflow Sentinel)**, **Target Engine confluence**, and **market breadth**.

To automate this workflow, the **🗺️ Cockpit** tab features a still **Alert Tape** backed by a **3.0-second louder brass bell chime 🔔**. The tape names where spot sits versus the gamma strikes. The text does not scroll; the bar holds still so the full message wraps and remains legible.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [● 14:15:22 IST] [⚖️ RANGE BETWEEN WALLS]                                              [🔔 Bell: ON] [🔔 Test] │
│ Outcome: Spot is inside the corridor ₹22,300–₹22,700. Directional edge is weak for naked buyers.        │
│ Drivers: Call gamma resistance at 22,700 and put gamma support at 22,300 frame a two-strike corridor.   │
│ Risk: Buying options inside the corridor incurs theta bleed without directional follow-through.        │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The alert tape speaks every state as **Outcome**, **Drivers**, and **Risk** for a naked option buyer:
- **Outcome**: The immediate directional takeaway (e.g., whether a breakout edge exists or if capital preservation applies).
- **Drivers**: The underlying positioning, gamma concentrations, walls, and orderflow dynamics.
- **Risk**: Explicit downside risk, theta decay exposure, or volatility traps.

A fresh Sentinel print (score at least 70, no older than 5 minutes) is written in front of the headline badge, for example **ABSORPTION · TESTING PUT-WALL CONCENTRATION**. That prefix is omitted during the pre-open and after the close. On an expiry day, a gamma-blast evaluation is added to the badge and body for that index's own listed expiry. It does not change the structural headline or the meaning.

### Alert tape headlines

| Headline | Meaning on the tape | When it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **EVALUATING MARKET REGIME** | Waiting for the first book. | Before the first payload arrives. |
| **MORNING BRIEFING — PRIOR-SESSION BOOK** | Yesterday's walls and flip, before the cash open. | Morning setup, before the cash pre-open. |
| **PRE-OPEN — PRIOR-SESSION STRUCTURE** | Auction is still on yesterday's walls until 09:15. | Cash auction, 09:00–09:15. |
| **SESSION CLOSED — EOD SETTLEMENT FROZEN** | Last published book. Nothing here is a live hedge. | After 15:30, or when the feed marks the session closed. |
| **BETWEEN WALL CONCENTRATIONS** | Spot is inside the corridor. The corridor is a map, not a fade. | Put wall below spot, call wall above, and spot is more than one strike step from both. |
| **TESTING PUT-WALL CONCENTRATION** | Within one strike step of the put wall. A touch is not a floor. | Spot is at the put-gamma strike and not yet a full step through it. |
| **TESTING CALL-WALL CONCENTRATION** | Within one strike step of the call wall. A touch is not a squeeze. | Spot is at the call-gamma strike and not yet a full step through it. |
| **BELOW PUT-WALL CONCENTRATION** | The put-gamma strike is already behind price. | Spot is at least one strike step under the put wall. |
| **ABOVE CALL-WALL CONCENTRATION** | The call-gamma strike is already behind price. | Spot is at least one strike step over the call wall. |
| **AT GEX MAGNET — NO WALL CORRIDOR** | Call and put concentration share one strike. There is no corridor. | Call and put concentration sit on the same strike, and spot is within one step of it. |
| **BELOW GEX MAGNET — CONCENTRATION BEHIND PRICE** | Spot is through the only gamma cluster. It is not a pin. | That single cluster is more than one strike step above spot. |
| **ABOVE GEX MAGNET — CONCENTRATION BEHIND PRICE** | Spot is through the only gamma cluster. It is not a squeeze. | That single cluster is more than one strike step below spot. |
| **WALL GEOMETRY UNCLEAR** | The strikes do not form a clean corridor around spot. | Walls are missing, inverted, or do not surround spot. |

Session headlines override the live geometry. Morning briefing wins over the auction, the auction wins over the close, and the close wins over a live wall reading.

### ⏱️ The 10-Second Trader's Triage Routine
When opening the terminal during a live trading session, follow this 4-step triage sequence to establish your market posture in under 10 seconds:

```
Step 1: Check 🗺️ Cockpit KPI Strip & Alert Tape

   ↳ T2 Confluence Score: > 65 = High conviction trend; < 50 = Sideways / No edge.

Step 4: Check 🧭 Market Intelligence

```

### The 4 Core Operating Scenarios: Mechanism & 15-Second Checklists

#### 1. How to Know When a Big Move is Coming (Volatility Expansion Imminent)
- **Book reading:** Absolute GEX is the volatility context. A low reading, in the research sample, lined up with wider forward vol. Spot sitting on one side of the call−put balance is a location. It is not a short-gamma accelerator, and it is not a 1.5× to 2× forecast.
- **What the labels mean:** POSITIVE means the call book is larger. NEGATIVE means the put book is larger. Neither word is a volatility forecast by itself.

##### ⚡ Quick 15-Second Checklist:
| Tab | Component / Card | What to Look For |
|---|---|---|
| **🗺️ Cockpit** | **Regime Classification & Net GEX** | • Regime reads **NEGATIVE** (put-heavy) or **POSITIVE** (call-heavy).<br>• Net GEX is the writer imbalance, call magnitude minus put magnitude.<br>• Spot relative to the flip is book balance, not a negative-gamma zone. |
| **🗺️ Cockpit** | **Expiry gamma watch** | • A high blast score means spot is near a gamma strike late in the session. It is a location, not a squeeze. |
| **📡 Options Analytics** | **ATM IV & IV Rank** | • IV compression followed by an IV rise on both wings is an implied-vol fact. It is not a dealer hedge. |
| **⚡ Sentinel Tape** | **Block Sweeps & Delta Spikes** | • A cluster of large prints is tape. Confirm it yourself. |

- **👉 Beginner Playbook:** If you trade a break, use a defined-risk structure and a stop beyond the gamma strike. The user confirms. No naked short is staged by this app.

---

#### 2. How to Know the Market is Trending (Bull or Bear Momentum)
- **Book reading:** A trend on the tape is CVD, breadth, and price holding a side of VWAP. Gamma strikes do not pin that move, and they do not chase it.
- **Rule of thumb:** Index, CVD, and sector breadth pointing the same way is a higher-quality tape read. Mixed breadth with a rising index is a weaker tape read.

##### ⚡ Quick 15-Second Checklist:
| Tab | Component / Card | What to Look For |
|---|---|---|
| **⚡ Sentinel Tape** | **CVD Regime & Cumulative Net CVD** | • CVD Regime shows **BUYER_DOMINATED** or **SELLER_DOMINATED**.<br>• The CVD line keeps making highs or lows.<br>• Buyer volume ratio stays above 55% or below 45%. |
| **⚡ Sentinel Tape** | **VWAP & EMA 9/21 Ribbon** | • Price holding outside the VWAP band is a tape location. |
| **🎯 Target Engine** | **T2 Confluence Dial** | • Dial above 65 is the engine's own confluence score. It is decision support. |
| **🧭 Market Intelligence** | **Sector Breadth & Net Contribution** | • Breadth above 8 of 11 in one colour, with net contribution in the same direction. |

- **👉 Beginner Playbook:** The user picks the option and the size. The cockpit does not send an order.

---

#### 3. How to Know the Market is Going to be Sideways (Chop / Mean Reversion)
- **Book reading:** High absolute GEX has meant calmer forward vol in the sample. The call-gamma and put-gamma strikes are concentrations. They are not a floor and a ceiling that a dealer book will defend.
- **When spot sits between those strikes and breadth is balanced,** a breakout is the lower-probability read. Size down. The user confirms. Do not fade to the magnet because a label says positive gamma.

##### ⚡ Quick 15-Second Checklist:
| Tab | Component / Card | What to Look For |
|---|---|---|
| **🗺️ Cockpit** | **Regime and net GEX** | • POSITIVE means call-heavy. NEGATIVE means put-heavy. Neither is a pin mode.<br>• Spot between the call-gamma and put-gamma strikes is a corridor on the map. |
| **🗺️ Cockpit** | **Strike versus the flip** | • A gamma strike above or below the flip is still a concentration. It is not support. |
| **⚡ Sentinel Tape** | **CVD Line & VWAP** | • CVD oscillating around zero and price returning to VWAP is a tape fact. |
| **🧭 Market Intelligence** | **Sector Performance** | • Balanced breadth is a chop read on the tape, not a dealer pin. |
| **📡 Options Analytics** | **Max pain** | • Max pain is the settlement strike that minimises writer payout. Distance to it is not a gravitation forecast. |

- **👉 Beginner Playbook:** Stops go beyond the gamma strikes, never at the magnet. The user confirms any structure. The app does not buy the put wall or sell the call wall for you.

---

#### 4. How to Know the Market is About to Break Out or Break Down
- **Book reading:** Price at a call-gamma or put-gamma strike, with absorption or exhaustion on the tape, is a location. It is not dealer inventory past an inflection, and it is not a cascade.

##### ⚡ Quick 15-Second Checklist:
| Tab | Component / Card | What to Look For |
|---|---|---|
| **🗺️ Cockpit** | **Gamma ladder** | • Spot at the call-gamma or put-gamma strike. A shift of the strike is a new map. It is not a short squeeze or a waterfall. |
| **⚡ Sentinel Tape** | **Absorption** | • Absorption at a gamma strike is tape. It is not a floor or a ceiling. |
| **📡 Options Analytics** | **Buildup** | • A PCR change is put open interest over call open interest. It is not a direction. |
| **🎯 Target Engine** | **Invalidation** | • A close through the invalidation level is the engine's own stop logic. The user confirms. |

- **👉 Beginner Playbook:** A close outside a gamma strike is a location. Size it yourself. The cockpit does not enter a squeeze or a cascade.

### 🛡️ Guardrail: Low-Quality Squeeze (Fakeout Detection)
- **Trap Setup:** Price is rising, but Sector Breadth is mixed (e.g. only 3 of 11 sectors green) and Cumulative Net CVD is flat or negative.
- **Institutional Reality:** Only 1 or 2 heavy-weight stocks are artificially pulling the index up while the broader market is being sold.
- **Alert Badge:** `⚠️ CAUTION: FAKEOUT`
- **👉 Action:** Stand aside or tighten stops. **DO NOT** chase this rally — high probability of a bull trap reversal.

- To prevent sensory overload from multiple confusing beeps, the system uses **ONE clean, loud, 3.0-second brass desk bell chime** synthesized natively via Web Audio (`987.77 Hz B5 fundamental` + 3 metallic partials).

## ⚡ Execution Architecture & Data Pipelines

### 1. Data Feed Hierarchy & Off-Hours Synchronization
- **Dhan WebSocket (`wss://api-feed.dhan.co`) [Primary Live Wire]:**
  - Streams real-time ticks, quotes, 5-level market depth, and contract open interest at sub-millisecond latency.
  - Feeds underlying spot indices (`IDX_I:13`, `25`, `51`), India VIX (`IDX_I:26`), near-month futures, and active option strikes.
- **Dhan REST API (`v2`) [Option Chain & History]:**
  - Fast snapshots of full options chains and 1-minute historical intraday bars for VWAP and ATR calculations.
  - **Off-Market Synchronization**: Outside market hours, Dhan sync stays completely idle to conserve API rate limits. It activates under exactly three triggers:
    1. A manual on-demand sync initiated from the UI.
    2. Scheduled morning sync at **06:30 IST**.
    3. Whenever a fresh broker access token is saved.
  - From **06:30 IST** onwards, the prior-session book is served directly from local disk snapshots without polling Dhan.
- **NSE India Scraper [Automatic Fail-Soft Fallback]:**
  - Scrapes official exchange option chains (`/api/option-chain-indices`) when broker connections are unavailable.
  - Historical journals (`futures_intraday_dhan` & `market_snapshot.db`) provide authentic EOD data with a strict zero-fabrication contract.

### 2. Strict Market Hours Enforcement
Trading and recommendation generation follow official Indian market session timings:
- **09:00 – 09:08 IST**: Pre-Open Order Entry Auction.
- **09:08 – 09:15 IST**: Order Matching & Pre-Bell 7-Minute Alpha Window (option shock calculations & audio discovery chime).
- **09:15 – 15:30 IST**: Normal Continuous Market Hours. Live option chains, recommendations, and OrderFlow active.
- **15:30 – 15:40 IST**: Cash Closing Auction Session (CAS) 30-min VWAP calculation & F&O extended close.
- **Outside Market Hours**: Recommendation generation is cleanly gated off. The cockpit seamlessly loads the last official closing state from the persistent SQLite market snapshot registry (`market_snapshots`), completely eliminating synthetic or fabricated price levels.
